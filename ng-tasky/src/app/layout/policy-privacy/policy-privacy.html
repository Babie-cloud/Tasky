Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Tasky, we collect basic personal information, including your name, email address, and authentication details (e.g., Google profile information if using OAuth).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain your Tasky boards and workspace.
  • Authenticate your identity and manage access permissions.
  • Send security alerts, updates, and support messages.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your task data and board details are shared only with users you explicitly invite to your workspace.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed password storage, to protect your personal data against unauthorized access.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal account data at any time through your profile settings or by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries or data requests, please reach out to privacy@tasky.com.
