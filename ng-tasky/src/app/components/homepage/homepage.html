How it works

From a blank board to a moving Kanban in three steps.

01

Create a board

Spin up a workspace for a project in seconds. Default lists — To Do, In Progress, Done — are ready immediately, and you stay the owner.

02

Invite your team

Send an email invitation and pick a role: admin, member, or observer. Access is scoped per board, so no one sees work they weren't invited to.

03

Drag, drop, done

Move cards across columns as work progresses. The order and status are saved instantly, for every collaborator, every time.

Everything a small team actually needs

Boards with real permissions

Owners, admins, members, and observers each see and do exactly what their role allows. Deleting a board stays reserved for its owner, even if an admin has every other privilege.

Kanban drag & drop

Reorder tasks inside a column or move them to another with a single gesture. Built on Angular CDK, so it feels instant on every screen size.

Invitations that just work

Add a teammate by email and they get a secure invite link. No shared passwords, no guessing who has access to what.

Sign in your way

Email and password, Google, or Facebook — every session is backed by a signed token, checked on every protected request.

Light or dark, your call

Switch themes from the navbar at any time. The whole app follows, not just this page.
